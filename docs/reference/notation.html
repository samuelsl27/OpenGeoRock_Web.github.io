5.6

Notation and glossary

Describes OGR Slip2D 0.1.235

The documentation uses one notation from the first chapter to the last. This page collects the symbols with their meaning and units, grouped by subject, and defines the terms of slope stability and of the program that the other pages use. Where a symbol has two meanings in different chapters, both are given.

5.6.1Conventions#

  • Units. SI throughout: lengths in m, forces in kN per metre of slope (the analysis is two-dimensional, in plane strain), stresses and pressures in kPa, unit weights in kN/m³, angles in degrees. The program stores and computes in these units whatever unit system its window displays.
  • Axes. xxx grows to the right and yyy upwards; a slope fails from left to right when the ground falls to the right.
  • Effective quantities carry a prime: c′c'c′, ϕ′\phi'ϕ′, σn′\sigma'_nσn′​.
  • Slices are numbered i=1…ni = 1 \dots ni=1…n; a symbol with the index iii belongs to slice iii, and vectors are set in bold (Wi\mathbf{W}_iWi​).
  • A dash (—) in the unit column marks a dimensionless quantity.
