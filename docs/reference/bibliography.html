5.7

Bibliography

Describes OGR Slip2D 0.1.235

The works cited in this documentation are listed here with their full references, grouped by subject. The formulas of the theory manual are attributed to their original sources, and the program's source code cites the same works where it implements them. A citation on any other page links to its entry here.
